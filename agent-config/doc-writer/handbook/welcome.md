# Welcome to Rokt Engineering

> **Source**: go/dev-handbook
> **Related files**: [onboarding.md](onboarding.md) (your first week), [business-context.md](business-context.md) (OKRs and metrics)

We build for speed, safety, and clarity. Our culture of Builders shows up in day-to-day mechanics: we prioritize outcomes over activity, choose small bets over sprawling plans, and write before we build so teams can align quickly and execute with autonomy.

AI is a co-pilot that accelerates judgment and craft, but it never replaces ownership; Builders still take the shot and own the result. We are committed to an AI-first approach, leveraging AI tools and automation to accelerate development, improve quality, and reduce toil wherever possible, while maintaining personal accountability for outcomes.

## Principles We Work By

We operate with alignment and autonomy. Clear direction, ruthless focus, and fast execution are expected at every level, with leaders acting as player-coaches who stay close to the work and amplify others through crisp written communication. This is the practical expression of our Builder DNA and healthy-org guidance, and it is why we default to lightweight, written alignment before we ship.

Outcomes guide our effort. Company OKRs set direction; we break work into shaped, time-boxed projects ("Bets") that allow teams to learn quickly, adjust from data, and avoid multi-quarter lock-in.

Trust is earned through rigor. We pair speed with transparent standards for ownership, reliability, security, and privacy so that partners, advertisers, and customers can rely on us in the transaction moment.

AI-First Solutioning. Builders are expected to integrate AI-assisted tools and workflows into their daily practice, assuming AI enablement from the start of every new capability.

## Choosing and Shaping Work

Rokt follows the planning process outlined in Shape Up (see [development-lifecycle.md](development-lifecycle.md)). We start from OKRs and shape the work into the smallest change that proves value. We write a Lightning Rod (a clear one-page proposal) or, for larger bets, a PRFAQ that frames customer (or client) value and answers the hard questions up front. Only then do we commit people and time.

Once a bet is approved into a cycle, work items are tracked in Jira. Builders assigned to that bet are expected to break down their own tasks and plan their approach to fulfil the requirements of that bet.

## Ownership Model

Every mission has a Single-Threaded Owner (STO) with a clear north-star metric. Every project has a Directly Responsible Individual (DRI) with authority and accountability for outcome. This clarity reduces hand-offs and makes decision-making faster.

**Personal ownership and accountability are core to our Builder DNA. Every builder is expected to own both the outcome and the process—taking responsibility for the quality, reliability, and security of what they ship, from shaping work to operating in production. This culture of end-to-end ownership empowers teams to move quickly while maintaining the high standards our customers and partners expect.**

It is your responsibility as a part of our engineering community to contribute back to our culture of engineering excellence (and this document as a representative thereof). Silent disagreement is not tolerated in our culture.

## Writing, Decisions, and Change Records

We default to writing because it scales alignment. Lightning Rods and PRFAQs create shared understanding quickly; ADRs record interface and architectural decisions in-repo with links to contracts, runbooks, and dashboards. This keeps the "why" discoverable long after the "what" ships.
